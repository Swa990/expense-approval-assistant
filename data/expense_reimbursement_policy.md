
# Company Expense Reimbursement Policy

Policy ID: EXP-POL-001
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Status: Active
Currency: INR

## 1. General Expense Rules

1. Employees can claim reimbursement only for legitimate business-related expenses.
2. Every reimbursement claim must contain supporting documentation.
3. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date.
4. Duplicate expense claims are not permitted.
5. Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.
6. The submitted expense amount must match the supporting receipt.

## 2. Travel Expenses

1. Travel expenses are eligible when they are related to official business travel.
2. The standard travel reimbursement limit is INR 25,000 per claim.
3. Travel expenses exceeding INR 25,000 require additional Finance Department approval.
4. Manager approval is mandatory for travel expense claims.
5. Valid travel receipts or booking confirmations must be provided.
6. Personal travel expenses are not reimbursable.

## 3. Medical Expenses

1. Medical expenses incurred for legitimate medical treatment are eligible.
2. The maximum reimbursement limit is INR 50,000 per claim.
3. Medical claims must include a hospital bill, medical receipt or pharmacy invoice.
4. Claims exceeding INR 50,000 require HR and Finance Department review.
5. Cosmetic and non-medical expenses are not reimbursable.
6. The employee must provide sufficient documentation to establish the medical expense.

## 4. Food and Meal Expenses

1. Business-related meals are eligible for reimbursement.
2. The maximum meal reimbursement limit is INR 1,500 per employee per day.
3. Meals exceeding INR 1,500 require manager approval and Finance review.
4. Itemized restaurant receipts must be submitted.
5. Personal meals without a business purpose are not reimbursable.

## 5. Accommodation Expenses

1. Accommodation expenses are eligible during official business travel.
2. The standard accommodation limit is INR 5,000 per night.
3. Expenses exceeding this limit require additional Finance approval.
4. Hotel invoices must contain the hotel name, transaction date and amount.
5. Personal accommodation expenses are not reimbursable.

## 6. Transportation Expenses

1. Official business transportation is eligible for reimbursement.
2. The maximum local transportation reimbursement is INR 3,000 per day.
3. Expenses exceeding INR 3,000 require Finance approval.
4. Taxi, bus, train and other transportation receipts must be submitted.
5. Personal transportation expenses are not reimbursable.

## 7. Office Supplies

1. Office supplies purchased for legitimate business use are eligible.
2. The maximum reimbursement limit is INR 10,000 per claim.
3. Expenses exceeding INR 10,000 require manager and Finance approval.
4. Original purchase receipts must be submitted.
5. Personal purchases are not reimbursable.

## 8. Approval Hierarchy

1. Manager approval is mandatory for all expense categories.
2. Expenses exceeding category-specific limits require Finance approval.
3. Medical claims exceeding INR 50,000 require HR and Finance review.
4. An expense awaiting additional approval must not be treated as approved.
5. Missing mandatory approval requires human review.

## 9. Missing or Invalid Documentation

1. Claims without mandatory supporting receipts must be placed under review.
2. Unreadable receipts require resubmission or manual verification.
3. Conflicting amounts between the submitted claim and receipt require review.
4. Missing transaction dates require manual verification.
5. Suspected duplicate claims require investigation before reimbursement.

## 10. Policy Interpretation

1. The category-specific policy must be applied to the relevant expense.
2. Where a general rule and a category-specific rule differ, the more specific category rule takes precedence.
3. This demonstration policy does not define every possible business exception.
4. Uncovered or ambiguous situations must be escalated for human review.
